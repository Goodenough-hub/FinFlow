# Category brand icon credits

Icons in this directory are used nominatively to identify third-party platforms
in the FinFlow app's category picker. Trademarks belong to their respective owners.

## Sources

Most files (`*.ico`, `*.png`) are each platform's own publicly served favicon
or apple-touch-icon, fetched from the corresponding official domain.
